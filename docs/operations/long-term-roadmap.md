# Storefront Ops: Long-Term Product & Engineering Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the multi-quarter innovation trajectory for **Storefront Ops**, expanding from multi-tenant fulfillment and inventory into autonomous AI operations, cross-border commerce, and edge warehouse networks.

---

## Q1: Autonomous AI Operations & Predictive Logistics
* **AI Autonomous Dispatch Agent**: Automatically balance warehouse pick waves and reassign fulfillment routes based on carrier cut-off deadlines.
* **Intelligent Restock Automation**: Autonomous purchase order creation with vendor confirmation loop and price negotiation tracking.
* **Support Copilot Pro**: Real-time voice and chat transcription directly generating return RMA authorizations and warranty replacements.

---

## Q2: Cross-Border Commerce & Global Customs Routing
* **Harmonized System (HS) Code Classification**: Automated AI classification of SKU catalogs for international customs duty calculations.
* **Landed Cost Currency Lock**: Real-time multi-currency FX hedging locking integer minor unit costs across 180 currencies.
* **International Carrier Mesh**: Direct decoupled integrations with DHL Express, DPD, Royal Mail, and Australia Post.

---

## Q3: Multi-Node Edge Warehouses & 3PL Network
* **Distributed Inventory Routing Engine**: Automatically route orders to the closest fulfillment node to minimize shipping zone costs and transit times.
* **B2B Wholesale Portal**: Dedicated bulk ordering, tiered price sheets, and credit terms invoicing for enterprise wholesale merchants.
* **Edge Terminal Sync**: Offline-first SQLite edge syncing for mobile warehouse pick carts and rugged handheld barcode scanners.

---

## Q4: Headless Omnichannel Point-of-Sale (POS) & Retail Mesh
* **In-Store Retail POS Adapter**: Unified inventory and checkout synchronization uniting brick-and-mortar stores with digital e-commerce channels.
* **End-to-End Serialized Lot Tracking**: Pharmaceutical and defense-grade batch tracking with cryptographic chain of custody.
* **Enterprise Custom Automation Studio**: Drag-and-drop workflow visualizer with sandboxed WebAssembly execution.
